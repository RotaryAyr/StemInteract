Our choice of AI tutors

STEMInteract regularly evaluates AI tutors for educational accuracy, interpretation of examination papers, use of official marking instructions and production of diagrams.

Following preliminary comparative testing of a Higher Economics question, Microsoft Copilot has been withdrawn from our recommended tutors because of concerns about the accuracy of its diagrams and its ability to verify its own answers. We will keep this decision under review.

All AI tutors can make mistakes. Always consult the official marking instructions.
